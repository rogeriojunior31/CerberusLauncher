# FAQ

## Is Cerberus free?

Yes. It's free and open source, under the [MIT license](../../LICENSE).

## Is it legal? Where do the games come from?

Cerberus doesn't host or download any game. Recomps are open-source projects that their developers publish on GitHub, GitLab or Forgejo, and Cerberus installs those official releases. Each recomp needs data from **a copy of the game you own**. Cerberus hands it over on your PC, and nothing is uploaded.

## Does Cerberus download BIOS files or ROMs?

No, never. For classics you point Cerberus to your own BIOS files and ROMs.

## Does it replace Steam or Epic?

No. Steam and Epic games still open through their own launchers. Cerberus lists them next to your other games and counts their play time.

## Can I use it with a controller?

Yes. [Big Picture Mode](../guide/big-picture.md) works with a controller, and the [in-game menu](../guide/in-game.md#the-in-game-menu) opens with **View + Menu** (or by holding **View**), and **View + RB** takes a screenshot.

## Does it work on Linux or macOS?

Not yet. The launcher is built for Windows. Some recomps have Linux and macOS versions, and the catalog already tracks them.

## Can I keep my recomps on another drive?

Yes. Change the **Recomps folder** in **Settings → Installation** for new installs. To move an existing recomp, use **Manage → Move to another folder or drive**.

## How do I move my saves to another PC?

Turn on [cloud saves](../guide/saves.md#cloud-saves) on both PCs with the same synced folder.

## Which languages does it support?

English and Brazilian Portuguese. More are planned; see [Translations](../development/translations.md).

## How do I request a recomp or report a bug?

Use **Request a recomp** in Discover, or [open an issue](https://github.com/rogeriojunior31/CerberusLauncher/issues/new/choose).
